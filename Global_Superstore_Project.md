# Global Superstore Sales Analysis

## Objective
To analyze sales performance and identify key trends, high-performing regions, and improvement areas using real-world business data.

## Tools Used
- Excel (Data Cleaning, Pivot Tables, Charts)
- Tableau (Interactive Dashboard)

## Key Insights
- Identified top-performing regions by revenue and profit
- Discovered most profitable product categories
- Analyzed monthly and yearly sales trends
- Highlighted underperforming segments for improvement
