# 📊 Global Superstore Sales Analysis

## 🎯 Objective  
To analyze sales performance and uncover key business insights related to revenue, profitability, and regional trends using real-world data.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used  
- Excel (Data Cleaning, Pivot Tables, Charts)  
- Tableau (Interactive Dashboard)  

---

## 📂 Dataset  
- Global Superstore dataset (public dataset)  

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed  
- Cleaned and prepared raw sales data in Excel  
- Created Pivot Tables to analyze sales, profit, and quantity  
- Performed time-based analysis (monthly and yearly trends)  
- Identified top-performing regions and product categories  
- Built an interactive Tableau dashboard for visualization  

---

## 📈 Key Insights  

- Top-performing regions contributed the highest revenue and profit  
- Technology category showed strong profitability compared to others  
- Sales trends indicated seasonal patterns across months and years  
- Certain regions and segments were identified as underperforming
